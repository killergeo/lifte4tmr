MOBILITY & RECOVERY • LIST OF STRETCHES

STRETCHING & MOBILITY GUIDE.
TRAIN WITHOUT PAIN.

Lifting heavy requires mobile joints, healthy tendons, and flexible muscle tissue. Use this comprehensive list of stretches for your pre-workout dynamic warm-up and post-workout recovery.

CHEST & SHOULDERS 30–45s HOLD / SIDE

Doorway Pec Stretch

Place your forearm against a door frame at a 90-degree angle. Step gently forward with one foot until you feel a deep stretch across your chest and front delt.

💡 Why it matters: Counters rounded forward shoulders from desk work and bench pressing, improving breathing and posture.
LATS & SPINE 45–60s HANG

Overhead Lat Hang & Decompression

Hang from a pull-up bar with relaxed shoulders and feet lightly touching the floor or suspended. Breathe deeply into your ribcage and lats.

💡 Why it matters: Decompresses spinal discs after heavy squats and deadlifts while lengthening the latissimus dorsi.

REAR DELT & ROTATOR CUFF 30s HOLD / ARM

Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch

Bring one arm across your chest at shoulder height. Use your opposite arm to gently pull your elbow closer to your torso without twisting your spine.

💡 Why it matters: Prevents shoulder impingement and keeps rear delts and rotator cuffs supple for overhead lifts.
HIP FLEXORS & QUADS 45s HOLD / LEG

The Couch Stretch

Back foot elevated on a wall or bench with your knee against the floor. Squeeze your glute and keep your torso upright until you feel a deep front hip stretch.

💡 Why it matters: The king of hip openers. Reverses tight hip flexors from sitting and eliminates lower back squat strain.
GLUTES & PIRIFORMIS 45s HOLD / SIDE

Pigeon Pose Glute Opener

From a push-up position, bring one shin forward perpendicular to your mat. Extend your back leg straight behind you and lower your chest forward.

💡 Why it matters: Releases deep gluteal tightness that causes sciatica and hip restriction during deep squats.
HAMSTRINGS 10 SWEEPS / LEG

Standing Hamstring Sweeps

Step one heel forward with toes pointed up and leg straight. Hinge at your hips, sweep your hands down toward your foot in a smooth arc, and stand up.

💡 Why it matters: A dynamic movement that activates hamstring flexibility without weakening muscle contractility prior to lifting.
FULL BODY / THORACIC 5 REPS / SIDE

World's Greatest Stretch

Deep lunge with both hands inside front foot. Rotate your inside arm up toward the ceiling, tracking with your eyes, then bring elbow back down.

💡 Why it matters: Simultaneously opens ankles, hips, groin, thoracic spine, and hip flexors in one movement.
ABDOMINALS & CORE 30s HOLD

Cobra Abdominal Stretch

Lie face down, hands under shoulders. Gently press up while keeping your hips on the floor, lifting your chest and gazing upward with soft shoulders.

💡 Why it matters: Lengthens the rectus abdominis and psoas after intense ab and core workouts.
LOWER BACK & SIDES 45s HOLD

Child's Pose with Side Reach

Kneel with knees wide and big toes touching. Sit your hips back onto your heels, reach your arms out long, then walk both hands to the left, then right.

💡 Why it matters: Releases compressive tension in lumbar vertebrae and gently stretches obliques and lat borders.
